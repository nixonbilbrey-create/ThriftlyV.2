Privacy Policy

This policy explains exactly what Thriftly does with your data — in plain language. It's short on purpose.

Version 2026-10-23

Last updated: October 23, 2026. Creating an account requires agreeing to this policy, and each account records which version it agreed to. Continued use after a revision means you accept the updated version; the version above changes with every revision.

Who runs Thriftly

Thriftly is a small app for people reselling secondhand items: you photograph something, the software identifies it, suggests a price and drafts listings for resale platforms. The operator is the person or entity that hosts the copy of Thriftly you're signing up on.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No payment information — the service costs nothing to run for you, and you bring your own Gemini key.
  • No contacts, location data, or anything off the one photo you choose to scan.

Third parties involved

  • Google (Gemini API). When you scan a photo or generate listings, your photo and the item text are sent to Google's Gemini service using your own API key, to identify the item and draft copy. This is a direct consequence of using the feature. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use inputs to improve its models under Google's own terms — treat your photos with that in mind.
  • Email provider. Verification and password-reset emails are delivered through the operator's SMTP provider, which processes the recipient address and message content to send them.
  • Hosting provider. The server (database, files, code) runs on cloud infrastructure the operator rents.

How long we keep things

Your account, drafts and photos are kept until you delete them. Verification and password-reset tokens are single-use, expire quickly, and are purged on a schedule. Deleting a draft removes its photo. Deleting your account (see below) removes everything at once.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account: while signed in, "Delete account…" erases your account, every draft, every photo, and all sessions immediately and irreversibly.
  • Disconnect your Gemini key: one button in settings removes it; the encrypted copy is deleted.
  • Delete drafts: individually or all at once, removes the draft and its photo.
  • Session control: logging out invalidates the current session; changing your password (via password reset) logs out every device.
  • Export: your listings are plain text in your drafts — copy them out at any time. If you need a bulk export of your photos, contact the operator.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; your Gemini key is encrypted at rest; sessions are bearer tokens that only work over the app's API; and the app sets standard browser protections (content-security policy, no-sniff, etc.). No system is perfectly secure, and the operator's server is the trust anchor — choose an operator you trust.

Children

Thriftly isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or deletion requests beyond the in-app tools: contact the operator of the Thriftly instance you're using, at the address given where you found this app. If you run your own instance, that's you — and you can read the entire codebase, because it's yours.
