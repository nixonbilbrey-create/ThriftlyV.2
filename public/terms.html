What Thriftly is

Thriftly is a tool for people reselling secondhand items. You upload a photo, it identifies the item and suggests a price and listing copy. It produces drafts — it does not post to any marketplace, does not sell anything for you, and is not affiliated with eBay, Vinted, Depop, Facebook Marketplace, or any other platform. You review and post listings yourself, under those platforms' own rules.

Thriftly is software run by the person or entity hosting the copy you signed up on ("the operator"). The operator may change, suspend, or discontinue the service.

The Privacy Policy spells this out.

Keys are stored encrypted, but no system is perfect — use a key you can revoke, and revoke it if you suspect exposure.
